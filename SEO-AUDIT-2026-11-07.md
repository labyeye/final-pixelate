# SEO Audit: pixelatenest.com (all 7,028 HTML pages)

**Date:** 7 Nov 2026. Follow-up to `FULL-AUDIT-REPORT.md` (6 Nov), run after the fixes made on 7 Nov.

## Method and limits
Every page in `final-pixelate/website` was parsed from source (title, description, canonical, robots, H1, schema, links, images, body text). Duplicate content was measured at block level: each text block (>=40 chars) is normalised (city/state name and digits masked) and counted across all pages. "Template share" = share of a page's blocks that appear on more than 20 pages. "Unique share" = share that appear on 3 or fewer pages.

**Not measured:** Search Console (indexation, impressions), backlinks, real rankings, Core Web Vitals, live-server headers and redirects. Without Search Console it is not confirmed which pages Google has indexed.

## Critical: duplicate content (unchanged, biggest ranking risk)
- **Location pages are 96-98% template.** Of 6,307 city pages, 6,272 have a template share of 80% or more, and 6,134 have a unique share of 5% or less.
- **Top-10 city pages** (170 pages, with the added "local context" block): template share 93%, unique share 3%. The block helps, but it is a small part of ~850 words.
- **Other 6,137 city pages:** template 97%, unique 0%. These carry only the state-level line and nearby-city links.
- **State pages:** 89% template.
- Google's scaled-content and doorway policies target exactly this pattern. The risk applies to the whole domain, not only these pages.
- **Fix:** only publish city pages that have real local content (client, project, pricing, local FAQ). For the rest, consolidate to state-level pages or noindex them. Add real content to the top 10-30 cities first.

## High
1. **Pages with no unique angle in `services/*-for-<industry>-business.html` (about 30 pages).** Each group is near identical (about 459 or 1,433 words), has only 2-3 inbound links, and has descriptions of about 95 chars. Merge into the parent service page or differentiate.
2. **`products/lead-management-software-bihar.html` has 1 inbound link.** It targets "lead management software in Bihar" and is a key page. Link it from the main nav, the NestLeads product page and the Bihar state page.
3. **`pages/portfolio.html` does not exist** but is linked 14 times ("View All Projects") from the industries pages. Create the page or point the links elsewhere.
4. **Orphan or near-orphan pages in the sitemap:** `pages/team-leadership.html` (0 inbound), `pages/photography.html` (1), `legal/delete-account.html` (1).
5. **2,862 indexable city-product pages are not in the sitemap** because their canonical points to the generic product page. This is consistent, but those pages will not rank. Decide per page: keep canonical and noindex, or make unique and self-canonical.
6. **Thin core pages:** `pages/contact.html` (93 words), `pages/pricing.html` (272), `pages/careers.html` (266), `services/hosting-maintenance.html` (604). Add substance, especially pricing, which is a high-intent page.

## Medium
- **H1:** 1,116 gym, clinic and academy city pages had one identical H1 on all 372 cities. **Fixed:** the city name is now in the H1. `legal/privacy-policy.html` has 9 H1 tags. `blog/index.html` has 2.
- **`blog/post.html`** was an indexable 17-word template with no canonical or H1. **Fixed:** noindex. The blog has 1 real page, so there is no content-marketing footprint yet.
- **Page weight:** `index.html` and `states/bihar/muzaffarpur.html` are over 200 KB.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `blog/index.html`. **Missing og:image** on 18 pages.
- **Titles over 60 chars (65 pages):** mostly `industry/*` pages and a few core pages (about, pricing, technologies).
- **Internal page `dashboard-add-work.html` and `portal.html`** are noindex but publicly reachable. Move them behind login.

## Low
- Image `width`/`height` and `loading="lazy"` are missing site-wide. Not changed automatically, because adding fixed dimensions can distort images where CSS sets only one dimension. Test on a template first.
- `404.html` title is 66 chars, which is irrelevant for a noindex page.

## Scanner notes (not site issues)
- Early output showed the homepage description as 22 characters. The scanner stopped at the apostrophe in "India's". The real description is fine (about 150 chars).
- A link to `pages/${member.socialLinks.instagram}` is JavaScript template text, not a real link.

## Fixed in this session (7 Nov)
1. Sitemap: removed 2,939 canonical-mismatch URLs, re-added 77 for top-10 cities, accurate `lastmod`, dropped `changefreq` and `priority` (4,156 URLs).
2. Removed 274 dead lead-page links (17 Bihar cities) and 2,144 broken `digital-marketing` links; fixed 5 industry links to the Bihar lead page.
3. Removed FAQPage markup from 6,532 location and state pages.
4. Added a styled "local context" section to 6,306 location pages (top-10 cities with real local facts).
5. Rewrote meta descriptions with varied wording: 6,306 location pages and 372 state-city pages, all unique.
6. Trimmed 4,100+ titles to 60 chars or less. Rebuilt the empty Nagercoil hosting page (it was 0 bytes).
7. Added the city name to 1,116 gym, clinic and academy H1s. Replaced the old brand name with NestAcademy. Fixed the invalid JSON-LD on `portfolio-details.html`. Set `blog/post.html` to noindex.
