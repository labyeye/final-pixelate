Refund Policy

Transparent and fair refund policies for all our services

Welcome to Decent Web Services LLP, we ensure transparency and fairness in all transactions. Please read our refund policy carefully regarding our services and products.

1. Service-Based Work

All payments made for services such as website design, software development, ERP solutions, mobile apps, or digital marketing are non-refundable once the project has started.

This policy ensures that our team can allocate resources effectively and begin work on your project with confidence.

2. Advance Payments

Advance payments are used for:

  • Resource allocation and team scheduling
  • Project planning and analysis
  • Initial development work and setup
  • Procurement of necessary tools and licenses

Therefore, advance payments are not eligible for refunds.

3. Project Cancellation

If a client chooses to cancel a project:

  • No refund will be issued for completed or ongoing work
  • The client will be responsible for all work completed up to the cancellation date
  • Any partial deliverables will be made available to the client
  • The client may be charged for additional costs incurred due to cancellation

4. Software & Digital Products

Refunds are not applicable for the following software solutions or licensed products:

  • ERP software
  • MLM software
  • CRM software
  • Nidhi or banking software
  • Any downloadable or licensed digital product
  • Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) subscriptions
  • API access and integration services

5. Exceptional Cases

Refund requests may be reviewed only in exceptional circumstances at the sole discretion of Decent Web Services LLP.

Exceptional cases may include:

  • Service failure on our part affecting project delivery
  • Technical issues beyond the client's control
  • Mutual agreement between both parties

Even if a refund is approved in exceptional cases, the client may not receive a full refund and may be charged for work already completed.

6. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved:

  • The refund will be processed within 7�10 working days
  • Refunds will be issued through the original payment method
  • The client will receive a confirmation email with transaction details
  • Processing time may vary depending on your bank or payment provider
