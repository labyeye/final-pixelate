Cookie Policy

Learn how Pixelate Nest uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience.

Last updated: April 2026  |  Pixelate Nest (Kalahanu Tech Studios LLP)

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the site. Cookies cannot run programs or deliver viruses to your device — they are simply data files.

How We Use Cookies

Pixelate Nest uses cookies to provide a better browsing experience and to understand how visitors interact with our website. We use the following categories of cookies:

1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function correctly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these cookies as the site cannot function without them.

2. Analytics & Performance Cookies

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use our site — such as which pages are visited most often and how users navigate between pages. This helps us improve the structure and content of our website. The data collected is aggregated and does not identify individual visitors.

  • Google Analytics (_ga, _gid, _gat cookies)
  • Google Tag Manager

3. Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your preferred language or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features. These cookies can also be used to remember changes you have made to text size, fonts, and other parts of web pages.

4. Marketing & Tracking Cookies

We may use cookies placed by our advertising partners (including Meta/Facebook Pixel) to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements on other sites. These cookies work by uniquely identifying your browser and internet device. If you do not allow these cookies, you will still see ads but they will be less relevant to your interests.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are set by third-party services we use. These include:

  • Google Analytics — website analytics and performance measurement
  • Google Tag Manager — tag management for tracking scripts
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel — conversion tracking and remarketing
  • Google Fonts — loading web fonts (may log your IP address)

We do not control these third-party cookies. Please refer to each provider's privacy policy for details on how they use your data.

How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.
  • Opt-out tools: You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Ad preferences: You can manage your advertising preferences at Google Ad Settings and through the Your Online Choices portal.

Cookie Retention

Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a defined period (typically 1 month to 2 years depending on the cookie type) or until you delete them manually.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date shown at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
