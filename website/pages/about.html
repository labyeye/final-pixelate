Our History

Way back in 1980s, the auspicious hands of late Shri Hanuman Mal Bothra laid the foundation stone of the KalaHanu group with organisations diversified into various leading sectors such as FMCG, media, telecom, commodity trading, automobiles and financial services. Since inception, KalaHanu has witnessed growth in phenomenal numbers not only in terms of further diversification, turnover and profitability, but also in garnering the trust and support of ever spawning employees and stakeholders. Today, grossing over more than Rs.1500 crore, the group has established a formidable position through consistent efforts and keen dedication, partnering its success with strong committed teams and stakeholders who are ever fervent and passionately infusing KalaHanu with vitality and energy, the quintessential ingredients for business growth.

What We Do

In 2026, Pixelate Nest was founded with a clear mission: to help ambitious brands navigate the digital world with confidence. We started by combining our passions for cinematic storytelling and cutting-edge web technology, creating a single, integrated agency where creative ideas can truly take flight.
Today, we partner with clients to build everything from high-performance websites to compelling video campaigns. At every step, you get access to a dedicated team with the strategic insights and technical expertise needed to turn your vision into a powerful digital asset.
Our Mission
Our mission at PixelateNest is to transform ideas into visually stunning, high-performing digital experiences through expert web development, creative video editing, and smart branding solutions.
Our Vision
We envision becoming a global leader in crafting innovative brand experiences, blending creativity and technology for businesses aiming to thrive in a connected world.
Our Goal
Our goal is to empower businesses with tailored digital strategies that increase visibility, accelerate growth, and leave lasting impressions.
