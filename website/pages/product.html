Frequently Asked Questions

Can your software work without internet (offline mode)?

Yes. Most of our products � Counter Billing, FMCG Retail, Hotel Management and School ERP � ship with an offline-first mode. Your billing, attendance and stock entries keep working even when your internet drops, and everything auto-syncs to the cloud the moment you're back online. You never lose a bill or a record.

How long does it take to deliver and set up the software?

For our ready-to-use products like GST Billing, Counter Billing and Library Management, you're typically live in 3�7 working days � including branding, GST setup and team training. Heavier deployments like School ERP, FMCG distribution or Hotel chains usually take 2�4 weeks depending on the number of branches, users and data migration involved.

Can the software be customised to match my exact workflow?

Absolutely. Every module � invoice template, fee structure, fleet log, lead pipeline or library card � can be configured to match exactly how your business already runs. If something doesn't exist out of the box, our team builds it as a custom module so you don't have to change the way you work. You can read more on our software development page.

Is my business data safe? Where is it stored?

Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, stored in India-region cloud servers with daily automated backups. Access is strictly role-based � your billing staff, manager and owner each see only what they're supposed to � and every sensitive action is recorded in an audit log. For sectors that need it, we also support PCI-DSS for payments and on-premise hosting on your own server.

Do you provide training for my team after delivery?

Yes � and it's free. We run live onboarding sessions in Hindi or English for your billing staff, teachers, receptionists or drivers, walk through real-life day-to-day scenarios, and leave you with quick-reference video guides. If a new team member joins later, we re-train them at no extra cost.

Can I migrate my existing data (Excel, Tally, old software)?

Yes. We import your existing item masters, customer ledgers, student records, room inventory, vehicle lists or stock data from Excel, Tally, Busy or any older system you're using. Our team cleans, validates and loads it for you so you go live without re-typing a single entry.

How is the software priced � one-time or subscription?

Both options are available. Most clients pick our monthly or annual subscription � it includes hosting, support and free updates. If you'd prefer a one-time purchase with self hosting on your own server, that's available for most products too. Detailed plans are listed on our pricing page.

What kind of after-sales support do you offer?

You get 24/7 support over WhatsApp, phone, email and remote desktop. Bug fixes, GST/compliance updates and security patches are pushed to your system automatically. If you ever want a new feature, custom report or extra module, our development team can build it for you on request.
