Frequently Asked Questions

Which website development agency should I choose in Kathua?

Pixelate Nest is a Muzaffarpur, Bihar-based agency that builds websites, software and apps for businesses in Kathua, Jammu and Kashmir. You get one team for design, development, SEO and support, and you can see progress at every step.

How much does website development cost in Kathua?

A basic business website starts from ₹15,000, e-commerce stores from ₹35,000 and custom web applications from ₹50,000. The final price depends on pages, features and integrations, and we share a clear quote after a free discovery call.

Can you work with a business in Kathua without meeting in person?

Yes. We work with Kathua clients over calls, WhatsApp and screen-sharing, so you don't need to travel anywhere. Your work, files and approvals stay in one place from kickoff to launch.

Which services do you offer in Kathua?

Website development, mobile apps, custom software and ERP, digital marketing and SEO, video production, UI/UX design and branding, and hosting and maintenance — for Kathua shops, schools, clinics, factories and startups.

Will my Kathua website rank on Google?

We build in technical SEO, Core Web Vitals and Google Business Profile setup so your site has a strong start in Kathua searches. Rankings depend on competition and ongoing work, so we never guarantee a #1 position — we track leads and calls instead.

Do websites include hosting and support?

Our websites include SSL, hosting setup and one year of free maintenance. After that you can continue with a simple maintenance plan or manage it yourself.

Can you build custom software or an app for my Kathua business?

Yes. We build billing and GST software, inventory, school and clinic management systems, and Android and iOS apps in React Native and Flutter, designed around how your business already works.

Can you create content for Jammu and Kashmir audiences?

We create content in Hindi and English. If your Kathua customers mostly use Urdu, Kashmiri, Dogri and Hindi, tell us during the discovery call and we'll plan the content accordingly.
