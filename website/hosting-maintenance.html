Domain Management & DNS Configuration

Your domain is your digital address — and getting it wrong can make your site invisible. We handle domain registration, annual renewals, and every DNS record that needs to be in place: A records, CNAMEs, MX records for email, and TXT records for verification. If you ever need to point your domain to a new host, add a subdomain for a campaign page, or enforce HTTPS with a valid SSL certificate, we've got you covered — no confusing dashboards, no guesswork.

Backend & Server Infrastructure Management

Your backend is the engine driving your website. A misconfigured server, an unpatched OS, or an API that silently fails can bring your entire platform down. We monitor your Node.js, Python, or PHP servers around the clock, apply OS and runtime patches proactively, and ensure your APIs are responding at healthy speeds. When something goes wrong — and it sometimes will — our team gets notified before you do, and we act fast.

Database, Security, Backups & Uptime Monitoring

Data loss and security breaches are not just technical problems — they're business crises. We run automated daily backups of your database (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Firebase, or others), store them securely off-site, and test restoration procedures regularly. Our security layer includes firewall rules, malware scanning, brute-force protection, and dependency vulnerability checks. Uptime monitoring ensures you're alerted in under 60 seconds if anything goes offline.
