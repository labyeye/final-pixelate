About NestDoc

One app for your clinic, from booking to billing

NestDoc is a cloud-based clinic management system for individual doctors and small clinics. A patient's visits, prescriptions, lab orders and bills sit on one record, so you can open a patient and see their history before they sit down. Doctors in Bathinda, Punjab can use NestDoc to run a clinic without paper files. Pixelate Nest sets it up online, adds your fee list and visiting hours, and trains your front-desk staff on a call.

Patient Records Visit history, notes and prescriptions on one patient record.
Billing Consultation, procedure and medicine bills with invoices.
WhatsApp Reminders Appointment reminders go out on WhatsApp, paid from a prepaid credit wallet.

Frequently asked questions

What is NestDoc?

NestDoc is clinic management software by Pixelate Nest for individual doctors and small clinics. It handles appointments, patient records, consultations and prescriptions, billing, lab orders and medicine stock.

Is NestDoc only for big hospitals?

No. It is designed for doctors who run their own clinic, from a single-doctor practice to a small team.

Can patients get appointment reminders on WhatsApp?

Yes. Reminders can be sent on WhatsApp. WhatsApp uses a separate prepaid credit wallet, and you choose whether to switch it on.

Do I need any special equipment?

No. NestDoc works in the browser and on a phone.

Is patient data kept private?

Each clinic has its own private data space and role-based access, so the front desk sees only what you allow.

How do I get started?

Book a free demo. We set up your clinic, add your fee list and visiting hours and train your staff on a call.

Is NestDoc available in Bathinda, Punjab?

Yes. Pixelate Nest provides demo, setup and support online for doctors and small clinics in Bathinda, Punjab. You do not need to visit an office, and we can help with your first setup on a call.
