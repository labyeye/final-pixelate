FMCG Retail & Distribution Software in Patna: What to Look For

FMCG distributors and wholesalers handle many items, schemes, routes and credit customers. The right software keeps stock, orders, billing, outstanding payments and salesman visits in one system, so growth does not turn into confusion. Here is what to check in Patna.

Who needs this in Patna?

  • FMCG distributors and wholesalers in Patna managing many SKUs and schemes
  • Retail chains and multi-counter stores
  • Businesses with salesmen and beat/route plans
  • Owners who struggle with outstanding payments and credit limits
  • Companies that need GST invoices and batch/expiry tracking

What affects the cost?

Pricing typically depends on scale and modules:

  • Number of users, godowns and billing counters
  • Modules: purchase, sales, inventory, schemes, salesman app
  • Batch, expiry and barcode handling
  • Credit, outstanding and collection tracking
  • Setup, data migration, training and support

How to choose the right provider

  • Confirm it handles your schemes, discounts and price lists
  • Check batch/expiry and low-stock alerts
  • Look for outstanding and credit limit controls
  • Ask about a salesman order app if you run routes
  • Make sure reports cover sales, stock, margin and GST

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Not modelling schemes correctly, which leads to billing disputes
  • Skipping stock count before go-live, so numbers never match
  • Leaving salesmen out of the system, so orders still come on paper
