About NestGym

One app to run your whole gym

NestGym is a cloud-based gym management system for single gyms, fitness studios and multi-branch chains. Every member has one record that holds their plan, payments, measurements and classes, while owners and managers track collections, dues and growth on the web dashboard. Gym owners in Kathua, Jammu and Kashmir can use NestGym to move off registers and spreadsheets. Pixelate Nest sets up your branch online, imports your member list and trains your team on a call, so there is no need to buy special machines or visit an office.

Member Records Profile, photo, health notes, emergency contact and full plan history in one place.
Fees & Invoices Collect full or part payments, send payment links and print GST invoices.
WhatsApp Reminders Renewal and due-date reminders go out on WhatsApp, paid from a prepaid credit wallet.
Web + Mobile App Front desk and trainers work from the phone, owners analyse from the dashboard.

Frequently asked questions

What is NestGym?

NestGym is gym management software by Pixelate Nest. It manages members, membership plans, fee collection, class bookings, trainers, lockers and reports from one web dashboard and a mobile app.

Who is NestGym for?

Gyms, fitness studios, yoga and dance studios, CrossFit boxes, personal-training studios and multi-branch fitness chains.

Do I need to buy any machine or hardware?

No. NestGym runs in the browser and on the Android app. You do not need special devices.

Can I move my existing members into NestGym?

Yes. You can import your member list from Excel, and our team helps with the first import.

Does NestGym send WhatsApp reminders?

Yes. Renewal and due-date reminders can be sent on WhatsApp. WhatsApp uses a separate prepaid credit wallet, and you choose whether to switch it on.

Can I manage more than one branch?

Yes. The Pro and Elite plans support multiple branches under one account, each with its own team and access.

Is NestGym available in Kathua, Jammu and Kashmir?

Yes. Pixelate Nest provides demo, setup and support online for gyms and fitness studios in Kathua, Jammu and Kashmir. You do not need to visit an office, and we can help with your first setup on a call.
