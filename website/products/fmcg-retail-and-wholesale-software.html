About FMCG Software

One platform to run your entire distribution chain

Our FMCG Retail and Wholesale Software is built for distributors, wholesalers and dealers who handle high-volume, fast-moving stock. Orders, billing, multi-warehouse inventory, schemes, returns, beat planning, sales targets and outstanding collections are connected, so field teams and the back office always work from the same numbers.

Order to Delivery Capture orders in the field, bill from the warehouse and track delivery status.
Multi-Warehouse Stock Batch and expiry-wise stock across godowns with transfers between them.
Schemes & Discounts Run trade schemes, slab discounts and free-goods offers without manual calculation.
Beat Planning Plan salesman beats and visits so every outlet is covered on time.

From order to invoice without re-entryField and counter orders in one flow

Salesmen capture orders at the outlet and the back office converts them into invoices with a click. Pending, billed and delivered orders are always visible, so nothing is lost between the field and the godown.

  • Order capture from the field and the counter
  • Order to invoice conversion
  • Pending and delivered order tracking
  • Salesman-wise order summaries

Stock you can trustBatches, expiry and multiple warehouses

Track every SKU by batch and expiry across all godowns. Stock transfers, damage entries and reorder alerts keep inventory accurate and stop expiry write-offs before they happen.

  • Batch and expiry-wise tracking
  • Stock transfer between warehouses
  • Reorder level alerts
  • Damage and expiry reports

Offers applied right, every timeTrade schemes without manual maths

Set up quantity slab discounts, free goods and period schemes once. The software applies them at billing and reports scheme cost, so you know exactly what each promotion is costing.

  • Slab, free-goods and value-based schemes
  • Scheme validity by date and brand
  • Automatic application at billing
  • Scheme cost and uptake reports

Cover every outlet, hit every targetPlan visits and measure achievement

Assign outlets to salesmen by beat and day, set monthly targets and compare achievement against plan. Managers see who is on track and which outlets have not ordered recently.

  • Beat and outlet assignment
  • Salesman-wise targets and achievement
  • Outlets not ordered recently
  • Visit and order reports

Decisions backed by numbersSee what sells, where and to whom

Analyse sales by brand, SKU, area, salesman and customer. Outstanding ageing, returns and margin reports help you decide what to push, what to stock and whom to follow up with.

  • Brand, SKU and area-wise sales
  • Outstanding and ageing reports
  • Returns and margin analysis
  • Excel and PDF export
