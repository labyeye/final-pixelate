About the Software

End-to-end FMCG distribution management — from order to delivery

Our FMCG Retail and Wholesale Software by Pixelate Nest is a comprehensive distribution management platform built for Indian FMCG businesses. Manage orders from your entire retailer network, track real-time inventory across warehouses, plan salesman beats, apply schemes and discounts, handle returns, and get full sales analytics — all in one system.

Mobile-first order managementOrders from field to warehouse instantly

Your salesman takes orders on the mobile app while visiting outlets — orders reach the warehouse instantly for processing. No phone calls, no manual entry, no delays. From order to dispatch in minutes.

  • Mobile order taking by salesman
  • Instant warehouse notification
  • Order status tracking for salesman
  • Credit limit check per retailer

Real-time inventory controlNever oversell or run out of stock

Track stock across all your warehouses in real time — when a salesman places an order, stock is reserved instantly. Set reorder alerts, track batch numbers and expiry dates, and prevent stock-out situations proactively.

  • Multi-warehouse stock tracking
  • Batch number and expiry tracking
  • Reorder level alerts per product
  • Stock transfer between warehouses

Beat planning & route salesMaximum outlets, minimum travel

Plan salesman beats by area and day — each salesman knows exactly which outlets to visit and in what order. Track daily visit completion, orders per beat, and productivity per salesman. Increase outlet coverage without adding headcount.

  • Area and day-wise beat assignment
  • Outlet visit checkin and checkout
  • Salesman productivity per beat
  • Missed outlet alerts to manager

Schemes, discounts & promotionsAuto-apply the right scheme on every order

Create and manage complex scheme structures — slab discounts, buy-x-get-y, seasonal offers, and retailer-specific discounts. The system auto-applies the right scheme when the salesman places the order — no manual calculations, no errors.

  • Slab-based and flat discount schemes
  • Buy-X-Get-Y and free goods schemes
  • Date-range and product-specific offers
  • Scheme performance reports

Deep sales analytics & reportingKnow what's selling, where, and by whom

Get product-wise, salesman-wise, area-wise, and brand-wise sales analytics. Compare performance by period, track target vs. achievement per salesman, and export any report to Excel in one click. Make decisions based on data, not guesswork.

  • Product and brand-wise sales reports
  • Salesman target vs. achievement
  • Area-wise and outlet-wise performance
  • Monthly, weekly, daily comparison reports
