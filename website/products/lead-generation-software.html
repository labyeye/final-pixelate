What is Lead Generation Software

Lead Generation Software by Pixelate Nest is a smart CRM and lead management platform designed for sales teams, marketing agencies, real estate firms, educational consultants, and businesses of all types. It captures leads from multiple channels including websites, landing pages, social media, and campaigns, and automatically assigns, tracks, and nurtures them through the sales pipeline. With real-time lead scoring, automated follow-up reminders, and detailed conversion analytics, your team closes more deals in less time with zero leads slipping through the cracks.
