Our Library Management Software is a complete automation solution for libraries of all sizes. It simplifies the management of books, journals, and digital resources. The system provides a user-friendly interface for both librarians and members. Easily catalog new books using ISBN data, manage member accounts, and track the circulation of books with our intuitive issue and return module. The software includes a powerful search function (OPAC) for members to find books quickly. Automated reminders for overdue books and fine management help in maintaining discipline and improving recovery. With detailed reports on circulation, member activity, and book inventory, you can gain valuable insights to enhance your library's services.

Access: Web-based & Mobile App

Features That Empower Your Library

Book Cataloging

Easily add and manage your entire book collection. Import book details automatically using ISBN numbers to save time.

Member Management

Maintain a detailed database of all library members, track their borrowing history, and manage memberships with ease.

Circulation Control

Streamline the process of issuing, returning, and renewing books. The system automatically calculates fines for overdue items.

OPAC (Online Catalog)

Provide your members with a powerful and easy-to-use online search portal to discover books and check their availability.

Barcode Integration

Use barcode scanners for quick and error-free check-in and check-out processes, enhancing efficiency for your staff.

Reporting & Analytics

Generate insightful reports on book popularity, member activity, and fine collections to make data-driven decisions for your library.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this software suitable for a small school library?

Yes, our software is highly scalable and is used by a wide range of institutions, from small school libraries to large public and academic libraries.

Can members access the library catalog from home?

Yes, the Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC) is web-based, allowing members to search for books, check their availability, and view their account from anywhere.

Does it support digital resources like e-books?

Yes, you can manage both physical books and digital resources, including e-books, articles, and links, all within the same system.

How is the data backed up?

Our cloud-based system performs automatic daily backups to ensure your library's data is always safe and secure. You can also perform manual backups anytime.

What is Library Management Software

Library Management Software by Pixelate Nest is a modern, cloud-based platform that digitizes and automates all library operations for schools, colleges, public libraries, and corporate knowledge centers. From book cataloguing and member registration to issue/return management, fine collection, and overdue reminders, our software streamlines every process. Librarians can manage thousands of books, periodicals, and digital resources from a single intuitive dashboard while members enjoy a self-service portal to search and reserve books online.
