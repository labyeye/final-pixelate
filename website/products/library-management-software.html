About the Software

A fully digital library — catalogued, tracked, and automated

Our Library Management Software by Pixelate Nest is a complete digital solution for school libraries, college libraries, public libraries, corporate libraries, and research institutions. It automates book cataloguing, member management, issue/return tracking, fine calculation, and reporting — eliminating paper registers, manual tracking, and errors completely.

Complete book cataloguingFind any book in seconds

Add your entire book collection with ISBN, author, publisher, edition, category, rack number, and quantity. Import via Excel or ISBN scanner. Students and staff can search the catalogue online anytime.

  • ISBN-based book entry and import
  • Category, genre, and rack location tagging
  • Online OPAC catalogue for member search
  • Multiple copies tracking per title

Fast issue & return with barcode10 seconds per transaction

Scan the member's card and book barcode — that's it. The system records the issue, sets the due date, and updates availability instantly. Return works the same way, with automatic fine calculation for overdue books.

  • Barcode and QR code scanning
  • Auto due date calculation by member type
  • Instant availability update on return
  • Book reservation and hold management

Member management & ID cardsEvery member tracked, every borrow recorded

Maintain complete profiles for all library members — students, staff, or public. Track their active borrows, history, fine balance, and membership status. Generate member ID cards with barcode or QR code.

  • Member profile with photo and contact
  • Complete borrow history per member
  • Member ID card with barcode/QR
  • Membership expiry and renewal management

Overdue fines & automated alertsNo manual follow-up needed

The system automatically calculates overdue fines per day based on your library's fine policy. SMS or WhatsApp alerts go out to members automatically when books are overdue — librarians don't have to chase anyone.

  • Configurable fine rate per day
  • Auto SMS/WhatsApp overdue alerts
  • Fine collection and receipt generation
  • Fine waiver and discount for special cases

Library analytics & reportsKnow your library inside out

See which books are most borrowed, who the most active readers are, fine collection totals, and inventory status — all from the analytics dashboard. Export any report to Excel in one click.

  • Most borrowed books and authors
  • Active vs inactive member reports
  • Fine collection and pending dues report
  • Book inventory and stock verification
