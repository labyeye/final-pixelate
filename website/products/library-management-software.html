About Library Management

One platform to digitise your entire library

Library Management Software by Pixelate Nest helps schools, colleges and institutions manage books, members and circulation. Cataloguing, barcode or QR scanning, issue and return, reservations, fines and reports sit in one system that librarians can learn in an afternoon.

Barcode & QR Scanning Issue and return books with a single scan and keep the catalogue accurate.
Instant Search Find any book by title, author, subject or ISBN in a moment.
Automatic Fines Due dates, overdue days and fines are calculated without a calculator.
Overdue Alerts Reminders reach members before and after the due date.

Every book, findable in secondsCatalogue that grows with your collection

Add books one by one or import your whole collection from Excel. Each title carries author, publisher, ISBN, category, shelf location and number of copies, with barcode labels you can print yourself.

  • ISBN, author, publisher and category fields
  • Multiple copies per title with accession numbers
  • Shelf and rack location
  • Barcode and QR label printing

Circulation without the paper registerScan the book, scan the member, done

Issue, return and renew books with a barcode scan. The system checks borrowing limits and due dates for each member type and keeps a complete history of every book and every reader.

  • Barcode and QR based circulation
  • Borrowing limits by member type
  • Renewals and reservations
  • Complete issue history per book and member

Know every readerStudents, staff and visitors in one list

Register students, teachers and public members with their class, department and membership validity. Import existing student lists and give each member a library card with a barcode.

  • Student, staff and public member types
  • Class and department details
  • Membership validity and renewal
  • Library card with barcode

Get books back on timeAutomatic fines and gentle reminders

Overdue fines are calculated by day based on rules you set, with waivers when needed. Members get alerts before and after due dates by SMS, WhatsApp or email.

  • Configurable fine per day by member type
  • Fine waiver with approval
  • Due-soon and overdue reminders
  • Overdue list for the librarian

See how your library is usedCirculation insights that guide purchases

See the most-borrowed titles, member activity, category-wise circulation and books that never move. Use real data to decide what to buy, what to retire and which classes read the most.

  • Most borrowed and never borrowed titles
  • Member-wise and class-wise activity
  • Stock and missing book reports
  • Export to Excel and PDF
