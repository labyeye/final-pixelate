About the Software

One platform to run your entire school — digitally

Our School Management System by Pixelate Nest is a complete ERP and CRM for schools, colleges and coaching institutes. Student and staff records, daily attendance, leave, exams and report cards, fee collection, expenses, timetables, notices and WhatsApp messaging all live in one cloud platform — with a separate, focused dashboard for each of nine roles.

9 Role-Based Dashboards Super admin, principal, teacher, student, parent, accountant, exam head and IT staff each see only what they need.
Automated Fee Management Invoices, partial payments, due dates and overdue status are calculated for you — balances never go out of sync.
WhatsApp Automation 15 ready templates for fee reminders, absence alerts, exam schedules, results, notices and transport delays.
Parent & Student Portal Parents follow attendance, results, fee dues and leave requests — with sibling switching in one login.

Complete student recordsEvery detail, one searchable profile

Replace paper files and scattered spreadsheets with a single student profile. Guardians, class and section, roll number, health details and documents stay together — and sibling accounts link to one parent login.

  • Detailed profile with academic, personal and guardian tabs
  • Attendance, marks and fee history on the same record
  • Parents linked to multiple children automatically
  • Login credentials created when a student is admitted

Attendance that parents see instantlyKnow who is in class, today

Teachers mark class attendance in seconds and the system takes care of the rest. Duplicate entries are blocked, leave requests feed straight into the register, and absent students trigger an alert to their parents.

  • Present, absent, late and leave states per student
  • Staff attendance alongside student attendance
  • Leave requests approved by teacher or principal
  • Biometric device logs visible to IT staff

Fees without the chasingInvoices, balances and receipts handled

Generate invoices per student, accept full or partial payments, and let the system work out the balance and status — paid, partial, unpaid or overdue — every time a payment is recorded.

  • Invoice numbering and line-item fee breakup
  • Partial payments with automatic balance update
  • Overdue status calculated from due date
  • Expense vouchers with approval for school outflows

From exam schedule to report cardMarks, grades and GPA calculated for you

Set up exams, enter marks class by class and publish results. Grade boundaries and GPA come from your own grading scale, so every result card is consistent and parents see only their own child's results.

  • Mid-term, unit test, semester and annual exams
  • Configurable grading scale with GPA and remarks
  • Batch marks entry for teachers
  • Class performance reports for the exam head

Reach every parent in secondsSend the right message automatically

Fifteen pre-built WhatsApp templates cover the messages a school sends every week — fee reminders, absence alerts, exam dates, results, holidays and PTA meetings. Pick an audience, send, and track the campaign.

  • Fee reminder and attendance alert templates
  • Exam schedule and result-declared messages
  • Transport delay and holiday announcements
  • Notices targeted by audience and priority
