About the Software

One platform to run your entire school — digitally

Our School Management System by Pixelate Nest is a comprehensive ERP for schools, colleges, and coaching institutes. It streamlines admissions, fee collection, attendance, timetabling, academics, library, transport, staff payroll, and parent communication — all from one cloud dashboard on mobile and desktop.

Admission & enrolment managementGo paperless from day one

Replace manual forms with a digital end-to-end process — from online inquiry to document verification, class assignment, and fee collection. Reduce admission chaos and give parents a smooth onboarding experience.

  • Online admission forms with document upload
  • Application tracking and status updates
  • Automatic class and roll number assignment
  • Student ID card generation

Fee management & online paymentsCollect fees without chasing parents

Set fee structures, generate invoices, accept online payments, and send automatic reminders for dues — all from the dashboard. No more manual fee registers or lost receipts.

  • Custom fee structures by class and category
  • Online payment integration (UPI, card, net banking)
  • Auto SMS reminders for pending dues
  • Digital receipts and financial reports

Smart attendance & parent alertsParents always know

Teachers mark attendance on the mobile app and parents receive instant SMS or WhatsApp alerts when their child is absent. Monthly attendance reports are auto-generated for every student.

  • Mobile app attendance for teachers
  • Instant SMS/WhatsApp alerts to parents
  • Monthly and termly attendance reports
  • Biometric integration support

Exam management & report cardsNo more manual mark sheets

Schedule exams, enter marks subject-wise, calculate grades automatically, and generate printable report cards — all in minutes. Teachers save hours; parents get results instantly.

  • Exam scheduling and hall ticket generation
  • Subject-wise marks entry with grade calculation
  • Auto report card generation and printing
  • Merit list and class rank analytics

Staff payroll & HR managementRun your school workforce with ease

Manage teacher and staff records, calculate salaries, generate pay slips, and track leave — all within the same system. No more separate Excel sheets for HR.

  • Staff profile and qualification records
  • Salary calculation and payslip generation
  • Leave management and approval workflow
  • Timetable and subject assignment
