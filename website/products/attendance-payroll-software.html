Category: HR & Workforce Management

Attendance & Payroll Software by Pixelate Nest is a comprehensive HR management solution designed to simplify and automate the most time-consuming aspects of workforce administration. Whether you run a small business or a large enterprise, our software ensures that employee attendance is tracked accurately and payroll is processed without errors, every single time. The attendance module supports multiple check-in methods including biometric integration, mobile app-based geo-tagged attendance, and manual entry, giving businesses the flexibility to choose what works best for their setup. Real-time attendance dashboards allow managers to monitor who is present, late, or absent at any given moment, making workforce visibility effortless. The payroll engine is built to handle complex salary structures including basic pay, HRA, DA, special allowances, PF, ESI, professional tax, and TDS deductions. Payslips are generated automatically and can be distributed via email to all employees at the click of a button. Leave management is fully integrated — employees can apply for leaves online, managers can approve or reject requests, and the system automatically adjusts the payroll accordingly. Holiday calendars, shift scheduling, and overtime calculations are all handled within the same platform. Compliance reports such as PF challan, ESI challan, and salary registers can be generated instantly for statutory filings. Role-based access ensures that HR managers, accountants, and employees each see only what they need to. Our software works both online and offline, so attendance data is never lost even in areas with poor connectivity. By choosing our Attendance & Payroll Software, businesses can reduce payroll processing time drastically, eliminate human error, and ensure full statutory compliance — all while giving employees a transparent and seamless HR experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Attendance & Payroll Software?

It is an integrated HR solution that automates employee attendance tracking, leave management, salary processing, payslip generation, and statutory compliance reporting for businesses of all sizes.

Which attendance methods does the software support?
