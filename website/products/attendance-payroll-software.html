Quick answers

What is NestHR in one line?

It is attendance, leave and payroll software that works together, so you stop doing the same work in registers, Excel and WhatsApp.

Do I need to buy special machines?

No. Employees can mark attendance on their phones with GPS or face recognition. If you already have biometric devices, NestHR can sync with them.

Does it work for more than one branch?

Yes. NestHR supports multiple branches and departments from one account.

Is it only for big companies?

No. It is made for growing businesses too. Pricing depends on the size of your team.
