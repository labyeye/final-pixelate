What is NestHR

NestHR by Pixelate Nest is a modern cloud-based HRMS software designed to simplify and automate human resource management for startups, businesses, agencies, schools, and enterprises. From employee attendance tracking and payroll processing to leave management, performance monitoring, and staff management, NestHR helps organizations manage their workforce efficiently through one smart and user-friendly platform. Built for growing businesses, NestHR improves productivity, reduces manual HR work, and streamlines daily operations with secure, scalable, and easy-to-use HR automation tools.
