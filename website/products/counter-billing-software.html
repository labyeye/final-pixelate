About Counter Billing

One platform to run your entire billing counter

Counter Billing by Pixelate Nest is a fast point-of-sale and inventory system for retail shops, stores and supermarkets. Billing, barcode scanning, stock, customers, discounts, GST and sales reports are connected, so every sale updates your inventory and books the moment the bill is printed.

Billing in Seconds Scan, add and print — queues at your counter get shorter from day one.
Works Offline Keep billing without internet; bills sync automatically when you are back online.
Live Stock Updates Every bill reduces stock, and low-stock items are flagged before you run out.
GST Ready GST-compliant invoices and tax reports generated without extra work.

Bill faster than the customer can queueBarcode scan to printed bill

Scan a barcode or search by name and the item is on the bill. Quantities, discounts and taxes are calculated instantly, and the bill prints on a thermal printer or goes out by WhatsApp or SMS.

  • Barcode scanner and quick-search support
  • Hold and resume bills during rush hours
  • Thermal printer and A4 invoice formats
  • Share bills on WhatsApp or SMS

Stock that updates itselfKnow what to reorder before you run out

Every sale reduces stock automatically and every purchase adds to it. Low-stock and expiry alerts keep shelves full, and stock reports show exactly what is selling and what is stuck.

  • Automatic stock reduction on every bill
  • Low-stock and expiry alerts
  • Purchase entry with supplier details
  • Item, category and batch-wise stock reports

Know your regularsHistory, dues and loyalty in one profile

Save customers at billing time and build a purchase history automatically. Track outstanding balances, reward repeat buyers and send offers to customers who have not visited in a while.

  • Customer profile with full purchase history
  • Outstanding balance tracking
  • Loyalty points and special pricing
  • Offer messages on WhatsApp and SMS

Run offers without calculator errorsRules applied at the counter automatically

Set item-wise, category-wise or bill-wise discounts and run festival offers or combo deals. The system applies them automatically, so cashiers cannot make mistakes or give unauthorised discounts.

  • Percentage and flat discounts
  • Combo and festival offers with date range
  • Discount limits per cashier role
  • Approval for manual price changes

Close the day in minutesReports your accountant will love

Daily sales, item-wise performance, payment modes and GST summaries are ready the moment the day ends. Export to Excel for your accountant and stop retyping bills into Tally.

  • Day-end summary by payment mode
  • GST-wise sales and tax reports
  • Cashier and counter-wise reports
  • Export to Excel and PDF
