Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of businesses can use this software?

Our Counter Billing Software is ideal for retail stores, restaurants, cafes, grocery stores, and any business that requires a fast and reliable point-of-sale system.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the software has a robust offline mode that allows you to continue billing and other operations without an internet connection. All data is synced to the cloud once connectivity is restored.

Can I manage inventory with this software?

Absolutely. The software includes a comprehensive inventory management module that helps you track stock levels, manage suppliers, and get alerts for low-stock items.

Is it easy to set up?

Yes, our software is designed for easy setup. Our support team is also available to guide you through the installation and setup process.
