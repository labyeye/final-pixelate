About the Software

Bill faster, sell more, manage smarter — from one counter

Our Counter Billing Software by Pixelate Nest is a complete point-of-sale and inventory management system for retail shops, medical stores, grocery stores, and general merchants. It's designed to be fast, simple, and accurate — even for shopkeepers with no technical background. Works fully offline with sync when internet is available.
