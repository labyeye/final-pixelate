About NestTransport

One platform to run your entire fleet

NestTransport is a cloud-based transport management platform for school buses, staff shuttles and small fleets. Vehicles, drivers, routes and stops, scheduled trips, passenger manifests, live GPS tracking, maintenance, fuel, documents and optional fee billing are all connected — so dispatchers always know what is moving, what is late and what needs attention.

Live Vehicle Tracking Watch vehicles move on the map in real time from GPS trackers or the driver's phone.
Routes, Stops & Trips Build ordered routes on a map, generate daily trips and avoid clashes of vehicle or driver.
Driver & Attendant App Trips, boarding, delays, breakdowns and emergencies reported from the phone — even offline.
Secure by Design Every organisation's data is isolated, with role-based permissions and a full audit log.

Every vehicle and driver in orderDocuments never expire unnoticed

Keep a clean register of vehicles, drivers, attendants and dispatchers. Insurance, fitness and licence documents are tracked with expiry dates and reminders, and vehicles under maintenance are blocked from trips automatically.

  • Vehicle registry with status and driver assignment
  • Document upload, verification and expiry alerts
  • Driver, attendant and dispatcher profiles
  • Vehicles taken out of service during maintenance

Plan routes once, run them every dayOrdered stops on an interactive map

Build routes visually with ordered stops, then duplicate, activate or archive them as your service changes. Each trip keeps a snapshot of the route plan it ran on, so history never changes retroactively.

  • Map editor for ordered stops
  • Route versioning with duplicate and archive
  • Distance and time entered per route
  • Same route reused across recurring trips

Dispatch without double-bookingSmart checks on every assignment

Create trips one by one or generate them from recurring schedules. The system checks that the vehicle, driver and attendant are free and available before a trip is confirmed, and handles replacements when plans change.

  • Numbered trips with a clear status flow
  • Overlap checks for vehicle, driver and attendant
  • Recurring schedules with safe bulk generation
  • Replace a driver or vehicle mid-plan

See every bus on the mapGPS from trackers or the driver's phone

Positions stream in live from supported GPS devices or from the driver's phone during an active trip. Duplicate and out-of-order pings are filtered, so the map stays accurate and the trip history stays clean.

  • Real-time map for running trips
  • Generic GPS tracker integration with per-device keys
  • Driver-phone location limited to the active trip
  • Boarding and drop-off events logged on every trip

Know your costs, collect your feesReports that export, invoices that reconcile

Fourteen built-in reports cover trips, utilisation, delays, fuel, maintenance, expenses and document expiry. Switch on the optional billing module to charge transport fees by route, stop or flat plan.

  • 14 reports with CSV export
  • Fuel logs with measured efficiency
  • Maintenance and expense tracking with approvals
  • Fee plans, invoices, part payments and receipts
