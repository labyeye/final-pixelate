About the Software

Complete fleet visibility — from route to driver to fuel

Our Transport Management Software by Pixelate Nest gives schools, colleges, corporate offices, and logistics businesses complete control over their vehicle fleet. Plan optimised routes, assign drivers, track vehicle location, alert parents on student safety, manage fuel logs, schedule maintenance, and get fleet performance analytics — all in real time.

Smart route planning & optimisationReduce travel time and fuel cost

Plan bus routes with stops, timing, and pickup points. The system optimises routes to minimise travel time and fuel consumption — reducing operational costs while improving punctuality.

  • Map-based route planning with stops
  • ETA calculation and schedule management
  • Passenger assignment per stop
  • Route overlap detection and optimisation

Student safety & parent alertsParents always know where their child is

When a student boards or exits the bus, parents receive an instant SMS or WhatsApp notification. School admin can see all students on board in real time — eliminating safety concerns completely.

  • RFID or QR-based student boarding tracking
  • Instant SMS/WhatsApp alerts to parents
  • Real-time headcount per vehicle
  • Late bus delay notifications to parents

Driver management & complianceKnow your drivers, track their trips

Maintain complete driver profiles — licence, experience, contact details, and trip history. Track licence expiry, police verification status, and assign drivers to specific vehicles and routes.

  • Driver profile with licence and documents
  • Licence expiry alerts and renewal reminders
  • Trip assignment and daily schedule
  • Driver performance and trip history

Fuel management & cost controlCut fuel costs by up to 30%

Log every fuel refill, track mileage per vehicle, and get cost-per-km analytics. Identify high-consumption vehicles, detect fuel pilferage, and set alerts when fuel consumption deviates from the norm.

  • Fuel fill log with quantity and cost
  • Mileage and cost-per-km reports
  • Fuel efficiency comparison by vehicle
  • Deviation alerts for unusual consumption

Vehicle maintenance schedulingZero unexpected breakdowns

Schedule and track all vehicle maintenance — oil change, tyre replacement, service dates, and fitness certificate renewals. Get alerts before due dates so maintenance never gets missed.

  • Service schedule and maintenance log
  • Pre-due alerts for service and fitness certificates
  • Maintenance cost per vehicle reports
  • Spare parts and repair history
