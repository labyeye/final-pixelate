About GST Billing

One platform for invoicing, accounts and GST

GST Billing by Pixelate Nest is an accounting and invoicing platform built for Indian businesses. Invoices, credit and debit notes, e-way bills, ledgers, expenses, bank reconciliation and GSTR-ready reports sit in one system, across multiple branches, with data your accountant can use directly.

GST-Compliant Invoices Correct CGST, SGST and IGST on every invoice, with HSN codes and place of supply.
E-Way Bill Support Generate e-way bill details directly from the invoice, with no re-entry.
Complete Accounts Customer and supplier ledgers, expenses and bank reconciliation in the same system.
Multi-Branch Ready Manage several branches and GST registrations from one login.

Invoices that are right the first timeTax split calculated automatically

Create tax invoices, bills of supply and quotations in seconds. The software picks CGST, SGST or IGST from the place of supply and applies the right rate for every item, with HSN or SAC codes on the invoice.

  • CGST, SGST and IGST calculated automatically
  • HSN and SAC codes per item
  • Quotations converted to invoices in one click
  • Share invoices as PDF on WhatsApp and email

Returns data ready before the due dateNo more month-end reconciliation panic

Sales and purchase data flows into GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B ready reports with invoice-level detail. Spot mismatches early and hand your accountant clean data instead of a pile of bills.

  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B ready reports
  • Input tax credit tracking
  • HSN-wise summary
  • Export to Excel and JSON for filing

Know who owes you, and how longReceivables and payables tracked live

Each customer and supplier gets a ledger with every invoice, payment and note. Ageing reports and payment reminders help you collect faster and avoid cash-flow surprises.

  • Customer and supplier ledgers
  • Receivable and payable ageing
  • Payment reminders on WhatsApp and SMS
  • Credit limits per customer

Dispatch without paperwork delaysE-way bill and notes from the same invoice

Prepare e-way bill details directly from a sales invoice, and handle sales returns and price adjustments with credit and debit notes that update tax and ledgers correctly.

  • E-way bill details from the invoice
  • Credit and debit notes linked to the original bill
  • Tax-correct handling of returns
  • Transporter and vehicle details stored

Understand your business in real timeProfit, expenses and bank in one place

Track expenses, reconcile your bank statement and view profit and loss, balance sheet and day book reports whenever you need them. Multi-branch businesses can compare branches side by side.

  • Profit and loss, balance sheet, day book
  • Expense tracking by category
  • Bank reconciliation
  • Branch-wise and consolidated reports
