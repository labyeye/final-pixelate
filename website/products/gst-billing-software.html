About the Software

GST compliance made simple — for every Indian business

Our GST Billing Software by Pixelate Nest is a complete accounting and invoicing platform designed for traders, retailers, manufacturers, and service providers in India. It automates GST invoice generation, GSTR filing, e-way bill creation, ledger management, and bank reconciliation — so you spend less time on paperwork and more on growing your business.

GST invoice generation100% compliant, every time

Create professional GST invoices for B2B, B2C, export, or service transactions. Apply the correct CGST, SGST, IGST rates automatically based on transaction type. Share via PDF, WhatsApp, or email instantly.

  • B2B, B2C, proforma, and tax invoices
  • Auto GST rate selection by HSN/SAC code
  • Credit note and debit note generation
  • Share via PDF, WhatsApp, or email

GSTR filing reportsCA-ready reports in one click

No more manual data entry for GST filing. Our software auto-generates GSTR-1, GSTR-2, GSTR-3B, and GSTR-9 reports from your transactions — in the exact JSON or Excel format required for portal upload or CA submission.

  • GSTR-1, GSTR-2, GSTR-3B auto-generation
  • JSON and Excel export for GST portal
  • Annual GSTR-9 reconciliation
  • ITC claim tracking and mismatches

E-way bill generationNo separate portal. No extra login.

Generate and manage e-way bills directly from within your billing screen. Our software connects to the NIC e-way bill portal via API — so you never have to log in separately or re-enter data.

  • Direct e-way bill generation from invoice
  • NIC portal API integration
  • E-way bill extension and cancellation
  • Bulk e-way bill generation for distributors

Customer & supplier ledgerKnow who owes you — and who you owe

Maintain complete ledgers for every customer and supplier. Track outstanding receivables, payments received, credit notes, and advance payments — with automatic ageing reports.

  • Customer and supplier ledger management
  • Outstanding receivables and payables
  • Payment reminders and follow-up alerts
  • Ageing analysis reports

Financial reports & accountingKnow your business numbers — always

Get P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, and cash flow reports at the click of a button. Share with your CA, use for bank loan applications, or analyse business performance anytime.

  • Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet
  • Trial Balance and day book
  • Cash and bank reconciliation
  • Excel export for CA and auditors
