About NestAcademy

One platform to run your entire sports academy

NestAcademy is a cloud-based academy management platform for sports clubs, coaching centres, dance and fitness studios. One record per student ties together attendance, fees, subscriptions and tournament history, while coaches mark attendance courtside on the mobile app and owners review reports and payroll on the web dashboard.

Face-Recognition Attendance Students check in by face scan; staff use a geofenced selfie or a biometric device at the gate.
QR Fee Renewals Parents pay by QR and upload proof; admins verify in-app and subscriptions renew automatically.
WhatsApp Alerts Check-in, fee-due, payslip and leave updates sent to parents and staff automatically.
Mobile + Web Dashboard Coaches work from the phone, owners analyse from the dashboard — same live data.

One profile for every studentSport, batch, guardians and history together

Enrol students in seconds or bulk-import hundreds from Excel. Each profile holds sport and experience level, batch and coach, multiple guardians, face enrolment and the student's full attendance, subscription and tournament history.

  • Multiple guardians with relation, phone and photo
  • Face enrolment for attendance recognition
  • Bulk import and export with an Excel template
  • Attendance, fees and events on one profile

Attendance as it actually happensFace scan, biometric or one-tap bulk marking

Capture attendance the way it happens on the ground. Students check in by face scan or biometric device, coaches mark whole batches in one tap, and every record feeds a WhatsApp alert to parents.

  • Present, absent, late, half-day, leave and holiday states
  • Bulk marking for a whole batch or team
  • Correction requests with late-approval workflow
  • Biometric devices over standard ADMS protocol

Payroll that follows attendancePresent days and overtime flow straight in

Staff attendance, leave, loans and payroll work together. Payroll is calculated from real present days and overtime, previewed, processed and marked paid — and payslips go out on WhatsApp as PDF.

  • Geofenced selfie check-in for coaches and staff
  • Payroll preview, process and mark-paid, in bulk
  • Loan and advance requests with approval and EMI
  • Leave approvals with automatic notifications

Courts and equipment, accounted forCapacity, bookings and inventory in one place

Manage courts, fields and halls with capacity and hourly fees, accept bookings with integrated payment verification, and keep track of equipment issued to students and coaches.

  • Facility capacity, hourly fee and schedule
  • Online booking with payment verification
  • Inventory with stock-in, stock-out and photos
  • Equipment assignment with return tracking

What is Pixelate Sports Manage

Pixelate Sports Manage by Pixelate Nest is a modern cloud-based sports academy and club management software designed to simplify and automate daily operations for sports academies, clubs, coaching institutes, and school sports programs. From student and player attendance to coach management, facility bookings, coaching plans, and subscription billing, Pixelate Sports Manage helps academies run efficiently through one smart, user-friendly platform.

Student Attendance

Track daily attendance for every student and player across batches and sports, with real-time dashboards showing who's present, late, or absent — across all your academy branches.

Employee & Coach Attendance

Mark and monitor attendance for coaches, trainers, and support staff so you always know who is on the ground and when.

Employee Management

Maintain a centralised digital directory of coaches and staff — roles, schedules, assigned batches, and contact details — securely stored and always accessible.

Student Management

Manage complete student and player profiles including batch history, documents, performance notes, and guardian contact information in one place.

Facilities Management

Manage grounds, courts, pools, and equipment bookings so your facilities are scheduled efficiently and never double-booked.

Coaching Plans Management

Create and assign coaching plans and batches, track student progress, and adjust training schedules as your academy grows.
