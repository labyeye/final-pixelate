Attendance software FAQs

What is attendance management software?

It records when each employee starts and ends work, applies your shift, late and overtime rules, and feeds the result into leave and payroll, so you stop maintaining paper registers and Excel sheets.

How can employees mark attendance in NestHR?

By face recognition, GPS with a geo-fence, a selfie, the mobile app or web portal, or a biometric device that syncs with NestHR. You can allow different methods for different teams or branches.

Can I use my existing biometric machine?

Yes. NestHR can sync punches from supported biometric devices, so you do not have to replace hardware you already own.

How does NestHR handle shifts and overtime?

You define shifts and overtime rules once. NestHR applies them to every punch, and overtime flows into salary calculation without manual entry.

What if an employee forgets to punch?

They send a regularisation request from the app. The manager approves or rejects it, and the attendance record is corrected with a trail of who changed what.

Does attendance connect to payroll?

Yes. Present days, leave, late marks and overtime feed directly into the salary run, including PF, ESI and TDS, so month-end payroll does not need re-entry.

Does it work for multiple branches?

Yes. Manage several branches and departments from one account, with branch-wise attendance views.

Can managers get alerts on WhatsApp?

Yes. NestHR can send HR and attendance alerts on WhatsApp, so managers do not need to open the dashboard to know what needs approval.

Pricing depends on team size and the modules you pick. See the pricing section on the NestHR page or book a demo for a quote.
