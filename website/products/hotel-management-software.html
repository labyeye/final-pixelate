About Hotel Management

One platform to run your entire property

Hotel Management Software by Pixelate Nest connects your front desk, rooms, housekeeping, restaurant, billing and staff in one cloud system. Real-time room availability, guest history, automated invoicing and revenue analytics help you fill more rooms and serve every guest better.

Live Reservations A visual room chart with real-time availability prevents double bookings.
Smooth Front Desk Fast check-in, check-out and guest folio billing with all charges in one place.
Housekeeping Tracking Room status and cleaning tasks update instantly between front desk and housekeeping.
Restaurant POS Room service and restaurant orders post to the guest folio automatically.

Never lose a booking againWalk-in, phone and online in one calendar

Create and modify bookings in seconds with rooms, rates and guest details in one form. A live room chart shows availability, so double bookings are prevented and last-minute rooms get sold.

  • Visual room availability chart
  • Walk-in, phone and online bookings
  • Rate plans, taxes and advance payments
  • Booking confirmations on WhatsApp and email

A front desk that moves at guest speedGuest ID, folio and payment handled together

Check guests in with ID details and room allocation, post charges during the stay, and check out with a clear itemised invoice. Guest history is saved, so repeat visitors are recognised instantly.

  • Guest registration with ID capture
  • Room allocation and changes
  • Itemised guest folio and GST invoice
  • Repeat guest history and preferences

Rooms ready when guests arriveLive status between desk and housekeeping

Housekeeping staff see which rooms to clean first, update status as they go, and the front desk sees it live. Maintenance issues are logged against the room so nothing is forgotten.

  • Clean, dirty, occupied and out-of-order status
  • Task assignment to housekeeping staff
  • Maintenance issue logging per room
  • Linen and amenity tracking

All charges on a single billRooms, food and extras together

Restaurant, room service, laundry and other extras post directly to the guest's folio. Accept advance, part and final payments in cash, card or UPI and generate GST-compliant invoices.

  • Restaurant POS linked to the room folio
  • Advance, part and final payments
  • GST invoice and payment receipts
  • Corporate billing and city ledger

Know what drives your revenueOccupancy and rate insights you can act on

Track occupancy, average daily rate and revenue per available room by day, month and room type. Compare booking sources and staff performance to price smarter and promote better.

  • Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR reports
  • Booking source analysis
  • Day-end and night-audit reports
  • Export to Excel and PDF
