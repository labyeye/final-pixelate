About the Software

One system to manage your entire hotel — front desk to back office

Our Hotel Management Software by Pixelate Nest is a comprehensive Property Management System (PMS) for hotels, resorts, guest houses, service apartments, and lodges. It manages room reservations, check-in/check-out, housekeeping, restaurant POS, staff, inventory, billing, and analytics — all in one cloud-based platform accessible from any device.

Real-Time Room ManagementSee room availability, status, and reservations at a glance — no overbooking ever.
Integrated Restaurant POSRestaurant orders, room service, and bar billing — all connected to the guest folio.
Online Booking IntegrationConnect to OTAs like MakeMyTrip, Booking.com, and your own website for instant reservations.

Room reservation & booking managementNo more overbooking

Manage walk-in and advance reservations on a live booking calendar. See room availability instantly, assign rooms, and send booking confirmations via SMS or email — all from the front desk screen.

  • Live booking calendar with drag-and-drop
  • Walk-in and advance reservation management
  • Auto booking confirmation via SMS/email
  • OTA channel integration (MakeMyTrip, Booking.com)

Housekeeping & room status managementClean rooms, happy guests

Assign housekeeping tasks to staff from the dashboard. Track cleaning status of every room in real time — dirty, clean, inspected, or maintenance. Reduce gaps between checkout and check-in.

  • Room status board (Dirty/Clean/Inspected)
  • Housekeeping task assignment to staff
  • Maintenance request tracking
  • Mobile app for housekeeping staff

Restaurant POS & room serviceAll charges go to one guest bill

Restaurant, bar, and room service orders are all linked to the guest's folio automatically. At checkout, all charges are consolidated into one invoice — no manual tracking or missed charges.

  • Table and room service order management
  • Auto posting to guest folio
  • KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket) printing
  • Bar and minibar inventory tracking

Guest billing & checkoutOne click, one bill

Generate a consolidated guest bill with room charges, restaurant, services, and taxes in one click. Accept cash, card, UPI, or corporate payments. Print or email GST-compliant receipts instantly.

  • Consolidated guest folio at checkout
  • GST invoice with auto tax calculation
  • Multi-payment mode acceptance
  • Corporate account and company billing

Revenue & occupancy analyticsKnow your hotel's performance daily

Track occupancy rate, Average Daily Rate (ADR), RevPAR, and revenue by room type, period, or channel. Make smarter pricing and staffing decisions with real data at your fingertips.

  • Occupancy rate and room night reports
  • ADR, RevPAR, and revenue analytics
  • Booking source analysis (walk-in vs OTA)
  • Monthly and annual P&L reports
